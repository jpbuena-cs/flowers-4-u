## What the image depicts

The image is a square, monochrome composition with a nearly black background. A light gray/off-white fabric glove sits in the center, angled slightly diagonally. Its middle finger is extended upward while the other fingers are folded toward the palm. The wrist ends in a broad cuff.

Visible details to preserve:

- Soft fabric material, not metal, armor, or a hard shell.
- Light and dark cloth shading across the fingers and palm.
- Stitched seams and narrow seam lines.
- Wrinkles and padded folds around the knuckles and palm.
- A broad, structured wrist cuff with horizontal bands and several narrow vertical details.
- A clear silhouette against the dark background.

The overall visual language resembles the familiar white performance/cartoon glove associated with Michael Jackson and Mickey Mouse, but the actual source's fabric texture, folds, seams, hand pose, and cuff should remain the reference.

## Source-of-truth rule

Use \`notebook/references/glove.png\` as the canonical visual reference for this project. Do not reinterpret the glove as metallic, tactical, armored, or robotic. Do not replace it with a generic cartoon glove.

The compressed ASCII map at \`assets/glove-map.txt.gz\` was generated from this same source artwork. Its crop and tonal settings are documented in [ascii-rendering.md](./ascii-rendering.md). When discussing or changing the conversion, compare it against \`glove.png\` rather than a remembered or generalized version of the subject.

## ASCII conversion priorities

Preserve the raised middle finger, curled fingers, palm folds, stitched fabric seams, cuff shape, glove highlights, and clean separation from the black background. These details matter more than adding effects that are not present in the source image.
